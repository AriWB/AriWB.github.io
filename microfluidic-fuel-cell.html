Microfluidic Fuel Cell

Developing microfluidic devices and exploring hydrogel barrier integration for a miniature fuel-cell concept.

Ongoing researchUndergraduate researcher · THEAM Lab

DLP 3D printing · PEGDA · Microfluidics

Project notes

ObjectiveMy contributionMethodologyResults & statusRepository →

Objective

Explore a microfluidic fuel-cell device intended for future organ-on-a-chip applications. The work focuses on device fabrication and PEGDA hydrogel barrier integration.

My contribution

My work focuses on designing and fabricating microfluidic chips using DLP 3D printing and investigating ways to integrate PEGDA hydrogel barriers inside the channels.

Methodology

  1. Develop microchannel geometries for fabrication by DLP 3D printing.
  2. Fabricate and inspect prototype chips.
  3. Investigate PEGDA barrier integration within the microchannels.
  4. Plan evaluation of fluid separation and device electrical performance once integration is established.

Results & current status

Device development and barrier integration are ongoing. No measured voltage, power output, validated biocompatibility, or successful organ-on-a-chip powering result is claimed.

Ongoing work

The current challenge is integrating the hydrogel barrier within the printed channels. Electrical and biological validation remain future work.

Scope & limitations

This is a high-level research overview. Experimental recipes, unpublished geometry, raw measurements, and lab-specific fabrication parameters are not included.
