Objective

Develop a vibration-based workflow for distinguishing healthy and faulty motor operation, with simulation and experimental validation as complementary parts of the capstone.

My contribution

As the Machine Learning & Code Lead, I develop MATLAB workflows for vibration analysis, feature extraction, and fault classification. I also participate in building and validating the team’s Simulink motor model, connecting the simulation work with our fault-detection approach.

Simulink model development

I contribute to building the motor model in Simulink and evaluating its behavior as part of the team’s modeling and validation work.

Machine learning & code

I lead the MATLAB code and machine-learning workflow, from processing vibration signals to training and evaluating classifiers.

Methodology

  1. Participate in building and validating the team’s Simulink motor model to support simulation-based fault-detection work.
  2. Inspect three-channel vibration records stored in MATLAB files.
  3. Remove the mean before FFT analysis and examine single-sided amplitude spectra.
  4. Extract crest factor, peak amplitude, kurtosis, dominant frequency, standard deviation, and RMS for each axis.
  5. Train a KNN classifier with a 70/30 holdout split and five-fold cross-validation for hyperparameter optimization within the training set.
  6. Evaluate held-out predictions using a confusion matrix, accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 with Faulty as the positive class.
  7. Explore FFT coefficient retention and inverse-FFT reconstruction using RMSE and normalized RMSE.

Current work

I am working on Simulink model development and validation alongside MATLAB signal processing and machine-learning evaluation. The capstone brings together simulation, vibration sensing, and experimental motor data.
