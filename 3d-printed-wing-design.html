3D-Printed Wing Design

Comparing spar locations to balance stiffness, stress, and mass in a printed semi-wing.

Objective

Design and fabricate a non-flying semi-wing while comparing three structural configurations: no spar, a spar at 25% chord, and a spar at 40% chord.

My contribution

I contributed as a member of the wing project team. The report is co-authored with Jude Boukarroum and Caleb Garverich; the design, analysis, and fabrication outcomes shown here are team results.

Methodology

  1. Use a NACA 2412 root airfoil transitioning to a NACA 0012 tip, with zero sweep and dihedral.
  2. Analyze three configurations with a fixed root and wing self-weight.
  3. Apply a total 5 N upward elliptical load over a 450 mm modeled semi-span at 25% chord.
  4. Compare predicted displacement, twist, nominal stress, and mass.
  5. Select the 25%-chord spar configuration and document the printed wing and fixture.

Results & current status

The selected configuration predicted 0.645 mm tip displacement and 0.214 MPa maximum nominal stress. The report records approximately 10.5% lower displacement and 9.3% lower nominal stress than the no-spar configuration, with modeled mass increasing from 233.8 g to 244.5 g.
